# Repo structure

**Status:** distributed default — becomes human source on adoption

Where things go in this repository — layout, placement, naming. Structure answers *where does this go*; it is consulted when something is added.

## Conventions

None recorded yet. Record them here as they are decided or discovered. A structure convention states where a kind of thing belongs and why — the why is what lets the convention be revised rather than merely obeyed.

Drift is silent by default. A convention that lives only in review comments is a convention the next contribution cannot check against.
